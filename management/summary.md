## Insurance (see insurance.json)
- Citizens: Coverage A must be < $700k in Manatee, so it's effectively unavailable here. You'll need private-client HO5 or E&S wind.
- NFIP caps at $250k building / $100k contents, so private excess flood is standard practice.
- Hurricane deductible is a percentage of the dwelling limit (at $3M+, insurers don't have to offer 2%). Plan for an umbrella and builder's risk too.
- Premium reducers: a wind-mitigation inspection (one example showed about 21% savings), an elevation certificate with 2+ ft of freeboard, and hardened post-2002 construction.
- Sourced ranges: Gulf-front VE combined $25k–$40k/yr. Luxury homes over $2M: $20k–$60k+.
